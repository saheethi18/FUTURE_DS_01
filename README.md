📊 Sales Performance Analysis (Task Description)
📌 Objective
This task focuses on analyzing sales data to extract meaningful business insights. The goal is not just to compute values, but to understand patterns, trends, and performance across products and regions.
📁 Dataset Overview
A sample dataset is used containing the following fields:
Date – Transaction date
Product – Product name (Laptop, Phone, Tablet)
Region – Sales region (North, South, East, West)
Sales – Revenue generated
Quantity – Number of units sold
⚙️ Steps Performed
1️⃣ Data Preparation
Created a dataset using Python dictionary
Converted Date column into proper datetime format for time-based analysis
2️⃣ KPI Analysis
Key performance indicators (KPIs) were calculated:
Total Revenue – Overall sales amount
Total Orders – Number of transactions
Average Order Value (AOV) – Revenue per order
👉 These KPIs help in understanding overall business performance.
3️⃣ Product Performance
Grouped data by Product
Identified Top-Selling Products based on revenue
👉 Helps businesses focus on high-performing products.
4️⃣ Regional Analysis
Grouped sales by Region
Compared performance across different locations
👉 Useful for identifying strong and weak markets.
5️⃣ Monthly Sales Trend
Extracted Month from Date
Analyzed monthly revenue trends
👉 Helps in identifying growth patterns and seasonality.
6️⃣ Data Visualization
Visual insights were created using:
📊 Bar Chart – Top products
🥧 Pie Chart – Region-wise sales distribution
📈 Line Chart – Monthly sales trend
👉 Visualizations make data easier to interpret and present.
📈 Key Insights
Laptop generates the highest revenue among products
Certain regions contribute more significantly to sales
Sales show variation across months, indicating trends
✅ Conclusion
This analysis demonstrates how raw data can be transformed into actionable insights. By performing KPI calculations, product and regional analysis, and trend visualization, we gain a clearer understanding of business performance.
